      Behind the scenes

      • The painting is one flat picture, hung up behind everything. Nothing in it is 3D.
      • The painter's camera. The 3D camera sits where the painter "stood", so 3D things line up with the brush strokes.
      • The walkmesh is an invisible floor traced over the planks, not the water. She can only stand on it, and it climbs the moot-hall steps.
      • Cut-outs are pieces of the painting stood up at their own depth. When she walks behind one, it covers her.

      Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom. You can keep walking with the keys.
